EXACT VENDOR-BINARY LAYOUT EVIDENCE
The vendor plugin differs from the public 1.8.3 source layout. Its indexed
stream stride is 0xd0 (208), not the source-derived 200 bytes. The stream array
starts at GstASFDemux+0x1a8, activated_streams is at +0x1ba8, flowcombiner at
+0x1bb0, and old_stream[0] at +0x1bb8.

Selected authentic libgstasf.so instructions:

  8e70: callq gst_flow_combiner_new@plt
  8e75: movq  %rax, 0x1bb0(%rbx)       # demux->flowcombiner

  97b2: movl  0x1a0(%rbp), %r15d      # demux->num_streams
  97b9: leaq  (%r15,%r15,2), %rax
  97bd: leaq  (%r15,%rax,4), %rdx
  97c1: shlq  $0x4, %rdx               # index * 13 * 16 = index * 208
  97c5: leaq  0x1a8(%rbp,%rdx), %rax   # &demux->stream[index]
  97e1: movq  %rbx, 0x1b8(%rdx)        # stream[index].pad at +0x10
  97ef: movw  %ax, 0x1c0(%rdx)         # stream[index].id at +0x18
  9805: movl  %eax, 0x1c8(%rdx)        # fps_known at +0x20
  97da: movq  %r12, 0x1d0(%rdx)        # caps at +0x28
  9829: movq  %rax, 0x1e0(%rdx)        # pending_tags at +0x38
  9886: movq  %rax, 0x1f8(%r13)        # payloads at +0x50
  9896: movq  %rax, 0x210(%r13)        # payloads_rev at +0x68
  990d: movl  $0x0, 0x1b0(%rbp)        # vendor field at stream+0x08
  9917: movl  $0x0, 0x1ac(%rbp)        # active at stream+0x04

For index 32, stream+0x08 equals demux+0x1bb0. Thus the instruction at 0x990d
zeros the low 32 bits of the live flowcombiner pointer. The later pad-pointer
store lands at old_stream[0]+0x00, rather than transplanting the pad directly
into flowcombiner as the unmodified public source layout suggested.
