## Canonical bzImage -> vmlinux -> embedded IKCONFIG chain

The bounded test extracts exactly one `./boot/bzImage-4.1.27-abl` regular member
from the hash-bound canonical archive and reads at most 16 MiB. Its boot protocol
0x020d setup header has setup_sects=30, setup bytes 0x3e00, payload offset field
0x26e, and payload length 0x50ebf7. The resulting absolute payload offset is
0x406e and the complete payload remains within the 5327072-byte member.

The actual compressed kernel payload is legacy LZ4 (`02 21 4c 18`), not gzip.
Before any output extension, the parser checks each decoded literal length
against both compressed-input bounds and remaining per-block output capacity.
It likewise checks each complete match length against remaining capacity before
copying. Overlap expansion uses bounded chunks of at most 64 KiB and never
constructs a repeated pattern. Before aggregate extension, each block output is
checked against remaining total vmlinux capacity. Tests force oversized literal,
oversized match, and aggregate overflow paths and require the pre-extension
errors. The parser also validates every token and match offset, 8 MiB block
bound, 32 MiB total-output bound, exact two-block consumption, and final
uncompressed-size footer 0xfa3828. Of all legacy-LZ4 magic occurrences, only
the setup-header-derived bounded candidate validates as an ELF kernel. It
produces the exact vmlinux size, SHA-256, and GNU Build ID above. This corrects
the review request's gzip assumption; the unique gzip stream belongs to the
embedded IKCONFIG, not to the outer kernel payload.

The decompressed vmlinux contains exactly one `IKCFG_ST` at 0x809860 and one
`IKCFG_ED` at 0x80fab2. The 25162 bytes between them begin with the image's only
`1f 8b 08` gzip magic. Bounded zlib/gzip decoding requires EOF, no unused or
unconsumed bytes, and at most 1 MiB output. It emits 113598 bytes, byte-identical
to `work/eu-260128/kernel.config`, with SHA-256:
  987268a1a4b333d67c219c63b690c3828811136a7a3b203eb6f20552e82dad56

## Generic usb-automount dispatch, direct mount and read-only retry

The mount FDE is [0x4c00,0x508d). It special-cases ntfs/exfat, then the generic
non-NTFS/exFAT branch copies the incoming filesystem type through exact "%s".
Consequently incoming type "iso9660" is copied as the mount(2) filesystem type:
    4c62: e8 79 d6 ff ff                callq 0x22e0
    4c7a: e8 61 d6 ff ff                callq 0x22e0
    4c81: 0f 85 49 03 00 00             jne 0x4fd0
    4fd8: 49 89 d8                      movq %rbx,%r8
    4fef: 48 8d 0d 58 29 00 00          leaq 0x2958(%rip),%rcx # 0x794e "%s"
    4ff6: e8 d5 d0 ff ff                callq 0x20d0

Initial generic flags are selected from exact 0xc0ed000a (configured rw,
MS_MGC_VAL|MS_NOSUID|MS_NOEXEC) or 0xc0ed000b (same plus MS_RDONLY):
    4cd7: b8 0b 00 ed c0                movl $0xc0ed000b,%eax
    4cdc: 48 89 44 24 18                movq %rax,0x18(%rsp)
    4f20: b8 0a 00 ed c0                movl $0xc0ed000a,%eax
    4f25: 48 89 44 24 18                movq %rax,0x18(%rsp)

The first direct mount uses the selected flags. Failure reaches a second direct
mount with explicit read-only flags 0xc0ed000b:
    4d59: 48 8b 4c 24 18                movq 0x18(%rsp),%rcx
    4d69: 4c 8d 05 3f 2b 00 00          leaq 0x2b3f(%rip),%r8 # "utf8=1"
    4d70: e8 fb d6 ff ff                callq 0x2470          # mount@plt
    4d75: 85 c0                         testl %eax,%eax

    4dec: b9 0b 00 ed c0                movl $0xc0ed000b,%ecx

    4df6: 85 c0                         testl %eax,%eax
    4df8: 0f 85 32 01 00 00             jne 0x4f30

## Bounded conclusion

Userspace can recognize ISO9660, preserve the established UUID byte route, and
attempt generic direct mount(2), including an explicit read-only retry. Exact
CONFIG_ISO9660_FS/UDF/BLK_DEV_SR `n` rows plus the tested stock module names and
aliases reject the named canonical ISO9660/UDF and SCSI CD-ROM routes. They do
not exclude inline, renamed, or vendor-equivalent implementations. Therefore
the broad exact recovered-stack ISO9660 and physical-optical mount routes remain
unknown, while the named canonical routes are false. The pre-mount UUID route
does not itself imply mount completion or a success D-Bus event.

Installed-unit kernel identity and extra modules are unknown. A materially
different installed runtime may classify differently. AppDMClient root-entry
processing remains conditional on a successful port-1 callback. Exact
mixed-case/dot ISO naming extensions and the AppDMClient-to-inner-AppUpgrade
package-base bridge remain unknown.
